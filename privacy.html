Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

Last Updated: January 1, 2025

1. Introduction

LazerMarkets ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, website, and services.

By using LazerMarkets, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

Our Commitment: We prioritize the security of your personal and financial information. We use bank-level encryption and never share your data with unauthorized third parties.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you register and use LazerMarkets, we collect:

  • Identity Data: Full name, email address, age, phone number
  • Location Data: Country of residence, IP address
  • Financial Data: Trading experience, source of funds, expected deposit amount
  • Account Data: Username, password (encrypted), AI Passkey
  • Transaction Data: Deposit/withdrawal history, trade history, profit/loss records
  • Verification Data: Email confirmation, phone verification (if applicable)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use our platform:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device model
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, features used
  • IP Address: For security and geolocation purposes
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device (see Section 7)

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Payment processors (NOWPayments) for deposit confirmations
  • Cryptocurrency networks for transaction verification
  • Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide Services: Process trades, deposits, withdrawals, and AI trading
  • Account Management: Create and manage your account, verify identity
  • Customer Support: Respond to inquiries, resolve issues
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access
  • Compliance: Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Improvements: Analyze usage to enhance our platform and AI algorithms
  • Communications: Send important updates, transaction confirmations
  • Marketing: With your consent, send promotional offers (you can opt out)

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information under the following legal bases:

  • Contract Performance: To fulfill our agreement with you (providing trading services)
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) requirements
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our services, prevent fraud, and ensure security
  • Consent: For marketing communications (you may withdraw consent anytime)

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data in the following circumstances:

5.1 Service Providers

We share data with trusted third parties who assist us:

  • Payment Processors: NOWPayments for deposit processing
  • Hosting Providers: Render.com for server infrastructure
  • Database Providers: MongoDB Atlas for data storage
  • Analytics: Google Analytics for usage insights (anonymized)

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation, including to comply with anti-money laundering laws.

5.3 Business Transfers

If LazerMarkets is acquired or merges with another company, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction.

6. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted using AES-256
  • Secure Connections: SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Password Protection: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • Access Controls: Strict internal access policies
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability scans
  • Monitoring: 24/7 intrusion detection systems

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Required for platform functionality (login, sessions) Session
Preference Cookies Remember your settings and preferences 30 days
Analytics Cookies Track usage to improve our platform Permanent
Security Cookies Prevent fraud and authenticate users Session

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect platform functionality.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services and maintain your account
  • Comply with legal obligations (typically 5-7 years for financial records)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Improve our AI algorithms (anonymized data)

After account closure, we may retain anonymized data for analytical purposes.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Restriction: Limit how we use your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Opt out of certain processing (e.g., marketing)
  • Withdraw Consent: For marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@lazermarkets.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. International Data Transfers

LazerMarkets operates globally. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for data transfers
  • Data processing agreements with service providers
  • Compliance with GDPR and other applicable laws

11. Children's Privacy

LazerMarkets is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we discover we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it immediately.

12. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., NOWPayments). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies before providing information.

13. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of material changes via:

  • Email notification to your registered address
  • Platform notification upon login
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date above

Continued use of LazerMarkets after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
